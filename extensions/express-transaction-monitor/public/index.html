About this demo

This page shows a live feed of card transactions, updating on its own without ever needing a refresh. These are simulated transactions, merchants, and card numbers, so nothing you see is a real account or a real charge.

A new transaction comes in about once a second, and a few get flagged as possibly fraudulent: the amount is unusually large, the charge came from an unexpected country, or it's an electronics purchase from outside the US.

Flagged charges show up below under "Needs review." Anyone looking at this page can handle a flagged charge in one of two ways: you can acknowledge it, meaning it looks fine, or you can escalate it, meaning it looks like real fraud. Escalated charges move down to the "Escalated" list, so that the team can follow up on fraudulent charges. When you handle a flagged charge, it will say who handled the charge and what decision they made, reflected for everyone who has the page open.

A note for whoever manages this app: you will want to configure the content's settings such that, on the "Runtime" tab, the Max processes and Min processes are both set to 1.
